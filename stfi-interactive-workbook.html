Welcome to Storytelling for Impact

This interactive workbook will help you harness the power of storytelling to amplify voices and values that strengthen inclusion and drive meaningful change across your campus community and beyond.

Whether you're sharing your own experiences, advocating for a program, or seeking to influence policy or culture, the right story told well can connect, inspire, and move people to act.

Lesson 2: The Science of Stories

▼

In this module, you'll explore what happens in the brain when listening to a well-told story. You'll learn why stories capture attention, evoke empathy, increase memory, and even boost perceived value.

2.1: Neuroscience of Stories

Storytelling may be an art form, but it's also a brain-based strategic skill set. In this lesson, you'll discover what happens in the brain when we hear a story and why narrative activates attention, memory, empathy, and trust in ways that data alone cannot.
